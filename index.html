A Little About Me

I’m a first-year BBA student specialising in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore. I’m still early in my journey, and that’s exactly what makes it exciting: every class, conversation and project opens up a new way of looking at how businesses work.

I’m fascinated by the digital business ecosystem, and by how companies use technology to reach people and create value. I’m especially curious about branding and marketing, and about why some brands become memorable while others are forgotten.

I enjoy understanding the thinking behind business strategies, and I’m learning to ask better questions about them. Alongside my coursework, I focus on continuous learning and building practical skills, including the HTML and CSS used to build this website.

Away from books, I practise judo. It has taught me discipline, respect and how to get back up after a fall, and I try to bring those habits into my studies too.

Beyond the Classroom: Judo

Judo means “the gentle way”. For me, its biggest lesson has been discipline.

Outside my studies, judo is where I’ve learnt the most about myself. Training teaches you that skill is built slowly, through repetition, patience and showing up even on the days you don’t feel like it.

Discipline

Regular practice matters more than motivation on any single day. Consistency is a habit I carry into my studies.

Respect

A bow begins and ends every session, to the mat, the coach and your training partner. Respect is part of the sport.

Resilience

You learn to fall safely before you learn to throw. Falling is part of progress: you get up and try again.

Focus

On the mat there is no room for distraction. That calm attention helps me when I study and solve problems.
